REAL CAMPUS HACKS

Spend Less Without Missing Out

Simple student hacks that preserve your lifestyle while saving thousands every term.

🍛

Smart Food Choices

Opt for college canteen thalis, packed home lunch boxes, and seasonal fruit stalls over delivery apps.

Typical Student Budget:₹30 – ₹70 / meal
🥤

Hydration & Drinks

Carry a steel reusable bottle, choose local fresh buttermilk or lemon water instead of ₹150 cafe drinks.

Typical Student Budget:₹5 – ₹20 / drink
📚

Education & Study

Borrow from the college library, purchase seniors' used textbooks, and utilize shared department Google Drives.

Typical Student Budget:₹0 – ₹150 / term
🚌

Commute & Passes

Apply for student city bus/metro concession passes, share campus auto carpools, or use campus bicycle racks.

Typical Student Budget:₹200 – ₹400 / month
